# Chicago Streets

A third-person, browser-playable recreation of Cloud Gate (the Bean), Millennium Park and the surrounding Chicago Loop at real-world scale. Built with three.js (r128); open `index.html` in a browser.

## Controls
- **Tab** get in / out of the black SUV (W/S drive, A/D steer, Space handbrake, Shift boost)
- **W A S D** move (relative to camera) · **Shift** run · **Space** jump
- **Mouse** look (click to lock pointer) · **Wheel** zoom
- **M** city map + fast travel · **H** hide help
- Touch: left stick to move, drag to look

## What's modelled
- Cloud Gate (20 × 13 × 10 m) with a walk-under arch and live mirror reflections
- Millennium Park on its raised deck: Grainger Plaza, McCormick Tribune Plaza, Wrigley Square peristyle, Crown Fountain, Jay Pritzker Pavilion + trellis, Great Lawn, Lurie Garden, Chase Promenade, Boeing Galleries
- BP Pedestrian Bridge over Columbus Dr, Nichols Bridgeway to the Art Institute Modern Wing terrace, Maggie Daley Park
- Street grid (Michigan, Wabash, Columbus, DuSable Lake Shore Dr, Randolph, Washington, Madison, Monroe) with signalled traffic, CTA buses and pedestrians
- Great Lawn picnics (seated and lounging people) and dogs of ten breeds: leashed walks, fetch, lounging and wandering
- Crown Fountain towers showing a custom photo face
- Lakefront: Lakefront Trail, limestone step revetment, animated lake water, Monroe Harbor moorings and breakwater, Chicago Yacht Club pier, DuSable Harbor docks, Navy Pier and Lake Point Tower on the skyline
- McCormick Tribune Ice Rink with skaters and spectators
- Walk-in Walgreens at Michigan & Washington (aisles, coolers, pharmacy, checkout)
- Drivable black SUV
- Michigan Ave streetwall and skyline landmarks (Aon, Two Prudential, BCBS, Aqua, 150 N Michigan, Willis, Trump)

Buildings are solid with visible, non-enterable storefronts.
